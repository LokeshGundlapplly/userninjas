Origin story

Arjun spent years watching product teams skip user research — not because they didn't value it, but because it was too slow and too expensive. A single UserTesting study could cost $3,000 and take five days.

He joined forces with Priya to rebuild the research stack from scratch: a global tester network with instant access, AI that replaces 90% of manual analysis, and pricing that startups can afford.

From the first line of code in early 2024 to 4,000+ teams today, the mission has stayed the same — make every product team as research-powered as the best in the world.
